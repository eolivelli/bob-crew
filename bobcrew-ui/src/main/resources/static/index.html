      You log in with your organization's identity provider; this UI keeps the login in memory, never in the browser, until you log out or stop it.

      Start the conversation over? The session stays open, in the same container.

      Keeping the files is useful when the workspace holds, say, a cloned repository.

      Close this session? Its agent stops, and its container and workspace files are deleted.

      Archiving keeps its transcript on the server, under Archived sessions, for you and those it is shared with to read later. Files in the workspace are not kept.
